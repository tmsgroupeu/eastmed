About EastMed

A maritime partner built for Egypt

EastMed Marine Agency is a specialist port agency and marine support provider operating across all Egyptian ports and the Suez Canal, one of the world's most strategically critical waterways.

We work with ship owners, operators, and fleet managers to coordinate every aspect of a vessel's call from a single point of contact, combining local expertise with the standards and resources of TMS Group.

Local Expertise

Deep knowledge of Egyptian port authorities, port procedures, and Suez Canal requirements.

Single Point of Contact

Agency, crew, chandling, repairs, and operational requirements managed by one team.

In-House Engineering Team

Licensed marine engineers available for immediate dispatch at any Egyptian port.

24 / 7 Readiness

Round-the-clock operational and technical support for urgent vessel requirements.

We deliver on our promises

Reliable local attendance, coordinated communication and technical readiness for every vessel call.
